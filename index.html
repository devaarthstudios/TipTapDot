TIPTAPDOT

TAP · CHAIN · SURVIVE

A fast one thumb arcade game. Popping dots keeps the clock alive, chaining them builds your combo, and every few levels a boss shows up to test you. Free to play, no ads, works completely offline.

WHAT IS IN THE GAME

One thumb, or ten fingers

Tap to pop. Drag a ball to carry it and smash it through the field. Up to ten fingers at once, and every finger multiplies your score.

Ten dot types

Gold alarm clocks, armoured targets, bombs, spikes, decoys, hold orbs and the line orb. They unlock one by one as you level up.

Boss fights

Every five levels a boss appears with a hard timer. Beat it before it fades out, or lose six seconds. From level 20 you face two at once.

Rewards that feel good

Combo multipliers, Overdrive with double score, a seven second NO DAMAGE shield, and a five second time freeze you can sweep clean.

Your name on the board

Type a name and it sits right next to your best score so you always know who you are chasing.

Respectful by design

No ads, no analytics, no accounts, no internet access. Just the game, a soundtrack built from scratch, and offline play.

How to play in four lines

  1. Tap dots to add time to the clock. The clock never stops draining.
  2. Chain dots in a cluster and keep tapping fast to raise your combo up to x9.
  3. Never tap the spiked ball or the red decoy. Each one costs you four seconds.
  4. Collect gold, overcharge the violet orb, and complete the cyan line to freeze time.
