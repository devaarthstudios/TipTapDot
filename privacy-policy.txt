TipTapDot Privacy Policy
Devaarth Studios
Last updated: September 16, 2026

TipTapDot is a mobile game made by Devaarth Studios. This page explains what
happens to your information when you play. The short version: the game does not
collect anything, and it cannot send anything anywhere.

1. Information we collect
We do not collect, request, or receive any personal information. There are no
accounts, no sign in, no email address required, and no contact list access.

2. Information stored on your device
The game saves a few things locally on your phone so it can remember your
progress between sessions. This information never leaves your device:
  * Your best score and best combo
  * The name you type for the high score screen
  * How many games you have played and how many dots you have popped
  * Your sound and music on or off settings
This data lives in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it. If you
uninstall TipTapDot, this data is deleted with it.

3. Internet access
TipTapDot does not ask for the Internet permission. Because of that, the app is
technically unable to send any information off your device, even if it wanted
to. The game works fully offline.

4. Permissions the app uses
  * Vibration. Used only to give a small haptic buzz when you pop dots, land
    combos, or hit a boss. You can turn this off in your phone settings at any
    time.
There are no other permissions.

5. Sharing and rating buttons
The game has Share App, Share Score, and Rate Us buttons. When you tap one of
these, Android opens the app you choose, such as a messaging app, a social app,
or the Google Play Store. Whatever you send is handled by that app under its own
privacy policy. We do not receive a copy, and we do not track whether you used
the button.

6. Advertising and analytics
There are no ads in TipTapDot. There are no analytics tools, no crash reporting
services, and no third party tracking software. Nothing is profiled and nothing
is sold.

7. Children's privacy
TipTapDot is not directed at children under 13. Since the game collects no
information at all, it also does not knowingly collect anything from children.
If you believe a child has somehow provided information to us, contact us and we
will help.

8. Changes to this policy
If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and
the date at the top will change. Continued use of the game after an update means
you accept the new version of this policy.
