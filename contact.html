Help us help you faster

For a bug report, please include:

  • Phone model, for example Pixel 8 or OnePlus 11
  • Android version, found in Settings, About phone
  • TipTapDot version, shown at the bottom of the main menu and on the Play listing
  • What you did just before it went wrong
  • A screenshot or screen recording if you have one

If the game crashed, the Android bug report or a short note about what was on screen helps a lot.
